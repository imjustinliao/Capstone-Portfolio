The User

Who You Are

College students ages 18 to 26. Students who don’t already have social circles that make IRL dating easy. People who care about real, natural vibes. You want to meet someone in real life, not just collect matches. Serene is for you.

What You Get From Serene

  • Less profile pressure: no resume-style bios or endless photo curation.
  • More authentic discovery: Polaroid-first profiles and location-based “who’s nearby.”
  • Faster path to IRL meetups; chats don’t lose momentum.
  • Safer structure: time-bounded windows and check-in options.
  • Real connections, not engagement metrics. A story you feel proud of.

How Serene Works (Your Experience)

Take your first Polaroid. Real, unfiltered, just you. Set preferences and availability. Share your location. Never miss your potential date. You discover profiles; the digital part stays lightweight until there is intent on both sides. Serene then pushes you toward safe, meaningful in-person interaction. When you and a match decide to meet, you confirm a meetup and use safety options during the meeting window.

Why This Feels Different

The Art of Serendipity. Relationships are natural, not intentional. Imagine meeting your future partner at the supermarket, park, or school library. You see friends and mutuals in the mix so matches feel grounded. The Polaroid capture signals that everyone is showing up as themselves. It’s not just another dating app, it’s a cultural movement to bring back GenZ romance.

Safety and Trust

Real photo capture reduces fake or heavily edited profiles. A time-bounded meeting window and QR check-in give both sides a clear frame for the meetup. You can optionally share location with trusted contacts. Serene keeps safety factual and avoids over-claiming; the goal is structure that supports real-world meetings.
