## A) Home canonical copy

**Tagline**
- "A dating app that brings GenZ romance IRL."

**Heading: The Dating App Fatigue**
- Swiping rarely turns into real dates
- Profiles are too perfect, not real
- Chats lose momentum
- Apps optimize for engagement, not relationships

**Heading: Why Serene?**
- Real profiles with in-person Polaroids
- Location-based matching based on who's nearby
- Friends and mutuals for more natural connection
- Built for meeting IRL, not endless matching or chatting

**Heading: Our Target Audience**
- College students ages 18 to 26
- Students who don't already have social circles
- People who want to meet and date in real life
- People who cares about real, natural vibes

**Heading: The Art of Serendipity**
- Relationships are natural, not intentional.
- We want people to have a real story about where they met.
- Imagine meeting your future partner at supermarket, park, and school library.

**Heading: How It Works**
- Create a profile
- Set preferences and availability
- Turn on location based discovery and notification
- Start swiping real profiles, matches are hidden
- Get notified when a match is nearby
- Decide whether you want to meet
- If so, date in real life, real time
