Serene

Meet your crush IRL.

Serene is a dating app for college students that skips endless swipes and helps people meet in real life. Serene takes the opposite approach: we keep the digital part lightweight, then push people toward safe, meaningful in-person interaction as soon as there is intent on both sides.

The Dating App Fatigue

  • Swiping rarely turns into real dates
  • Profiles are too perfect, not real
  • Chats lose momentum
  • Apps optimize for engagement, not relationships

Why Serene?

  • Real profiles with in-person Polaroids
  • Location-based matching based on who’s nearby
  • Friends and mutuals for more natural connection
  • Built for meeting IRL, not endless matching or chatting

Our Target Audience

  • College students ages 18 to 26
  • Students who don't already have social circles
  • People who want to meet and date in real life
  • People who care about real, natural vibes

The Art of Serendipity

Relationships are natural, not intentional. We want people to have a real story about where they met. Imagine meeting your future partner at supermarket, park, and school library.

How It Works

  1. Create a profile
  2. Set preferences and availability
  3. Turn on location based discovery and notification
  4. Start swiping real profiles, matches are hidden
  5. Get notified when a match is nearby
  6. Decide whether you want to meet
  7. If so, date in real life, real time
